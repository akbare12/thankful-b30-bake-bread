# Bake Bread — a warm loaf for the judge

One slow act of thanks: dough kneaded by hand, left to rise, baked till
the crust turns gold, and set warm on the judge's table, still steaming.

Thank you. Morning found you reading, and no warm loaf has reached you yet. I kneaded the dough slow, let it rise, and baked it till the crust turned gold. Here it is on your table, still steaming, waiting to be cut. Break off a piece. The smell of fresh bread will stay in this room long after the last crumb is gone.
